Bedtime Guard Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

In short: Bedtime Guard does not collect, share or sell any information. It has no internet access, so nothing it sees or stores ever leaves the phone it's installed on.

Bedtime Guard is a parental tool that stops videos and other media from playing past bedtime. A parent installs it on a child's phone and sets a bedtime schedule. At bedtime, the app pauses whatever is playing, shows a bedtime screen, and keeps media paused until wake-up time. This policy explains what the app uses on the phone to do that, and what it keeps.

No internet access, no data collection

The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. Android's app backup is turned off for Bedtime Guard, so its settings aren't copied to cloud backups either.

What the app uses on the phone, and why

Permission or accessWhat it's used for
Notification access Lets the app see which apps are playing media and tell them to pause or stop at bedtime. The app also reads notifications from Bark apps only (not the Bark parent app) and writes their title and text to the on-phone diagnostic log, to learn whether Bark announces bedtime. Other apps' notifications are not read or stored.
Installed apps Lists video apps so the parent can turn off picture-in-picture, and lists apps for the "Allowed at bedtime" setting. App names and icons are shown on screen only.
Display over other apps Opens the bedtime screen on top of a video that's playing.
Alarms & reminders Starts bedtime at the scheduled time.
Notifications and full-screen alerts Shows the bedtime alert, including when the phone is locked.
Start at boot Sets up the next bedtime again after the phone restarts.

What the app stores on the phone

  • Settings: the bedtime schedule, the list of apps allowed at bedtime, and a list of apps that have played media on the phone (used to suggest apps for that list).
  • Logs: a diagnostic log of each bedtime (times, app names, what was paused) and a parent log of unlocks, wrong codes and setting changes.
  • Parent lock: if the parent turns it on, a secret shared with the parent's authenticator app is kept in the Android Keystore, where it can't be read back out, and the recovery code is kept only as a one-way hash.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on the phone.

Children

Bedtime Guard is used by parents on their children's phones, and parents control its settings. The app does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else, and it does not show ads or offer purchases.

Keeping and deleting data

Stored settings and logs remain on the phone until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling Bedtime Guard deletes everything it stored. The diagnostic log can also be cleared from within the app. The parent log can't be cleared from within the app, so a parent can see if settings were changed.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
